      How is this result calculated?

      The eight questions measure four separate areas of Generative UI readiness: use case fit, technical readiness, control and safety, discoverability. Each area is scored on its own from 0 to 6. A strong area does not cover a gap in another one, which is why we do not show a single total score.

      Your result type comes from evaluating the thresholds across those four areas in a set order. Your strongest answer then sets the ground you have today. Your two lowest answers, or on an exact tie the two highest-risk areas, set the controls to secure first during the pilot.

      Soft Commitment and UserGuiding wrote the questions for this project. The result is not an industry benchmark and not an automated product review. It is a self-assessment, built so your team can talk about Generative UI readiness under the same headings.

      The result is calculated from the eight answers you pick and nothing else. No product connection, no code analysis, no customer data.
